05 / Common questions

Quick answers
before we talk.

The questions I get asked most. If yours isn't here, just send it over.

01 What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly small businesses, service providers, and growing teams that need a stronger online presence — barbershops, clinics, education providers, agencies, and founders starting something new. If you're not sure if we're a fit, just reach out and we'll figure it out.

02 How long does a project usually take?

A focused landing page can take 1–2 weeks. A multi-page business site or a lead generation system with follow-up flows usually takes 3–5 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly we can gather content and feedback.

03 What does a project cost?

Every project is different, so I give a clear estimate after we talk through your goals, scope, and timeline. No hidden fees. If the budget doesn't match the scope, I'll tell you upfront and suggest a smaller starting point.

04 Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. After launch, I offer a short support window for fixes and small tweaks, and I'm available for ongoing maintenance or new features if you want to keep improving the site over time.

05 Do you work remotely?

Yes — I'm based in Kathmandu, Nepal and work with clients remotely by email and video calls. Time zone differences haven't been an issue so far, and I keep communication clear and consistent.

06 I'm not sure what I need yet. Can you still help?

Absolutely. If you know what isn't working but you're not sure what to build, that's a great starting point. Send a short message about your business and where you're stuck, and I'll suggest the most practical next step.
